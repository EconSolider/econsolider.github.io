### Introduction to Intermediate Macroeconomics [[GitHub]](https://github.com/EconSolider/introduction-to-intermediate-macroeconomics-2025)

An undergraduate course in **dynamic macroeconomics with microfoundations**. Across 14 lectures, it builds from two-period household consumption–saving and labor-supply problems up to infinite-horizon optimization, the Real Business Cycle (RBC) model, consumption-based asset pricing, growth theory, and monetary economics (IS–MP–PC).

Lecture slides are provided in **English, Chinese, and Japanese**, together with figures, data, and accompanying Dynare/MATLAB code for the computational models.

### Introduction to Econometrics and Causal Inference [[GitHub]](https://github.com/EconSolider/introduction-to-econometrics-and-casual-inference-2025)

An introductory undergraduate course in **econometrics and causal inference** (originally *Data Analysis D / データ解析D* at Kwansei Gakuin University), pairing statistical theory with hands-on Stata exercises. The 13 lectures cover simple and multiple regression, fixed effects, difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, synthetic control, regression discontinuity design, and maximum likelihood estimation.

Lecture slides are available in **Japanese, Chinese, and English**, with datasets and Stata code for each session.
